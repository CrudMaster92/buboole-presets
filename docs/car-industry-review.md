# Car Industry — source review

Prepared 2026-10-21T06:35:14.036920+00:00. 66 company identities and 74 native monitors. 16,879 records and 16,855 distinct source URLs were observed across dated validation runs; these are not current worldwide vacancy totals or net-new Buboole/FreeHire roles.

Every included recipe ran through the canonical application scraper runtime. The serialized recipes expand only the defaults already applied by that runtime; both input and effective recipe hashes are recorded in car-industry-20261021.json. Browser sources also ran in the public collector child process with its HTTPS, request, byte and time budgets. No credentials, personal filters, private tracker data or upstream crawler code are included.

All role families are requested. Regional, subsidiary and bounded boards remain explicitly partial. Complete traversal of one board does not imply worldwide company coverage. Some sources provide listing metadata or only bounded description enrichment. Group boards (including Bosch, GPC and Volkswagen) can contain non-automotive or subsidiary roles. Overlapping monitor records are not counted as additional unique vacancies.

Advance Auto Parts, AutoZone, BorgWarner, Dana, Ford, General Motors, Goodyear and Lear reuse their existing monitor IDs. Tesla reuses its Germany Automation monitor with explicit public-browser bounds. Distinct regional systems retain separate monitors. Collection membership references monitors; it never embeds recipes. New companies retain initials where no company artwork was reviewed.

## Held sources

- KG Mobility: the discovered KGM NineHire careers site returned HTTP 429 with a Vercel Security Checkpoint. A separately tested old recruitment hostname fails TLS hostname verification. Neither is bypassed or published as a verified recipe.
- Great Wall Motor: its verified European site exposes a careers/HR contact section rather than a current vacancy feed. The sampled FreeHire JobsDB posting is indirect regional evidence, not a reusable manufacturer-owned worldwide scraper.

These are research outcomes for a defined 68-company roster, not a census of every automaker, vehicle brand, dealer or supplier worldwide. Other regional/group boards remain potential additions. Tesla worldwide, Korean HQ systems, and separately hosted subsidiaries remain explicit gaps even when a regional monitor is included.
